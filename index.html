Bakery orders

Bakery tracker guide

Check the pickup schedule and book helpers if needed. Fetch today’s lecture messages. Review the inbox, then choose Accept all and go to next day. After Sunday, close the remaining pickup days. Select a day in the pickup calendar to see its orders.

Each order has one product. Two calendar days of notice apply to orders, changes, cancellations, and helper bookings. Cupcakes cost $40 and take 30 minutes per dozen. Cakes cost $120 and take 2 hours each. A $100 helper adds 4 hours to the 8-hour oven day.

Open this HTML file directly in Chrome. It includes the lecture’s orders and inbox. Progress stays in the browser for this copy. Downloading or uploading the HTML shares a fresh copy of the exercise.
